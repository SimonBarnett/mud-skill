## LOCKED — Inspect fighting tree

1. `skills fighting` — lists melee/ranged/points branches.
2. `skills fighting.melee.sword` (example) — drill into weapon family you carry.
3. After gear change, re-check before hard targets (`discworld-fight-consider`).

## LOCKED — Train without burning XP

- **Greg** newbie area — weapon/defence TM (`discworld-fight-newbie-greg`).
- **Taskmaster** while using the weapon type in real fights — watch `hskills`.
- Guild **teachers** in advance rooms — follow game signs; primaries depend on
  warrior **specialisation** (`discworld-mud`).

## LOCKED — Spending XP on fighting

- In guild advance room: `cost fighting` / `cost primaries` then `advance <skill>`.
- Ask human before pushing primaries past comfort budget.
- Prefer **`advance`** over **`teach`** for cost when guild allows (public player guides).
